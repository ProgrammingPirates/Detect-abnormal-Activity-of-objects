📡 1. Single Direct Signal (LOS) vs Multiple Signals (Multipath):
• Single Direct Path (Line-of-Sight - LOS): The straight electromagnetic wave traveling directly from Router antenna to Receiver antenna ($d_0$).
• Multiple Reflected Paths (Multipath - NLOS): In an indoor room, the transmitted radio wave strikes walls, ceiling, doors, furniture, and the human body. Each reflection creates an independent copy with distinct attenuation $\alpha_i$ and propagation delay $\tau_i$:
h(t) = Σ α_i · e^(-j φ_i) · δ(t - τ_i) • Constructive vs Destructive Interference: When multiple signal copies arrive at the receiver antenna, their peaks and troughs superimpose. If two paths arrive 180° out of phase, they cancel each other out (Multipath Fading).
• Why RSSI Fails: Standard Wi-Fi RSSI compresses all multiple paths into a single gross number. If a human moves slightly, paths cancel destructively, making RSSI wildly noisy and useless for subtle vital sensing.

📶 2. Single Frequency vs 56 Multiple OFDM Subcarriers:
• Wi-Fi 802.11n/ax divides the 20 MHz channel into 56 orthogonal subcarrier tones (each 312.5 kHz apart).
• Channel State Information (CSI): Measures the complex transmission matrix $H_k = I_k + j Q_k = A_k e^{j \theta_k}$ across all 56 subcarriers simultaneously.
• Frequency Diversity: Even if subcarrier #10 is in a deep destructive fade, subcarrier #24 experiences constructive interference! Human chest micro-displacements (±0.8 cm) modulate subcarriers #18 to #36 with high SNR.

👥 3. Single Person vs Multiple Persons (Spatial Separation):
• Single Person: Produces a single dominant Doppler frequency shift $f_d = 2v/\lambda \approx 0.25$ Hz corresponding to 15-16 BPM respiration.
• Multiple Persons: Signals scatter across distinct concentric Fresnel Ellipsoid Zones: ||r_Tx - p|| + ||r_Rx - p|| - d = n(λ/2). By applying Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT), different Doppler clusters separate independent human bodies in 3D space!
